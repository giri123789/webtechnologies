Famous Places of India

Explore the beauty of Incredible India!

Nature | History | Beaches | Adventure

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is a famous white marble monument and a symbol of love.

2. Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jaipur is known as the Pink City. It is famous for its forts, palaces and colourful markets.

3. Goa

Goa is famous for beautiful beaches, sunsets and coastal scenery.

4. Kerala

Kerala is famous for green landscapes, coconut trees and peaceful backwaters.

5. Chennai

Chennai is famous for its temples, beaches and cultural heritage.

6. Tirumula

Tirumula is famous for its natural beauty and serene environment.

7. kashmir, jammu kashmir

Kashmir is famous for its scenic beauty, including mountains, lakes and gardens.

8. Mysore, Karnataka

Mysore is famous for its palaces, gardens and cultural heritage.
